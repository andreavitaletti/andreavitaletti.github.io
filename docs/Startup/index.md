## The exam

### Individual assignments (typically three):
 1. Introduce yourself in a measurable way.
 2. Present your favourite startup from Y Combinator and explain why you find it compelling.
 3. Identify and present a startup that has emerged from a research group at Sapienza.
### Group project: building a startup.
 The method is more important than the idea itself: a fantastic idea that has not been properly validated has marginal value. The goal is therefore to learn how to identify a problem, formulate hypotheses, test them with real users, gather evidence, and iteratively refine the business idea.
